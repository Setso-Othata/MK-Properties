About MK Properties

Built for Gaborone's property market.

MK Properties is a Gaborone-based property consultancy offering estate agency, property management, valuation and project management services under one roof. The agency works across residential and commercial property — helping owners sell and let, and helping buyers and tenants find the right property with less friction.

The practice is registered with Botswana's Real Estate Advisory Council (REAC) and is active daily with the Gaborone property community it serves.
